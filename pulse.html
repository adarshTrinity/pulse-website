What Is Pulse

A device the size of your palm.
The power of a full motion studio.

Pulse is a compact wireless motion tracker built by Trinity XR for film, VFX, and game production. Clip it to any object — a camera, a prop, an actor's costume — and its 6-axis sensor streams live positional data directly to your screen, your engine, and your pipeline.

No bulky rigs. No post-production surprises. No reshoots. Just real-world motion, translated to your digital world in real time.

The Problem

VFX reshoots cost millions and weeks of time.

Film crews shoot complex sequences with no live feedback on how motion will translate to post-production. The result? Everyone guesses. And guesswork is expensive.

Tracking failures are discovered too late. Reshoots are scheduled. Budgets spiral. Creative vision gets compromised at every step.

Shooting Blind

Crews have no real-time visibility into how camera motion will behave in post-production. Every take is a gamble.

Costly Delays

Tracking failures are discovered in the VFX suite, not on set — when it's far too late and far too expensive to fix.

Creative Compromise

Directors and VFX supervisors must settle for what was captured rather than what was envisioned. The gap kills great work.

The Solution
